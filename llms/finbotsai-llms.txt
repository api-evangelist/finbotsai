# Finbots.AI

> Finbots.AI (finbots.ai) is a Singapore-headquartered fintech offering CreditX, a no-code, AI-powered credit risk platform that lets banks and lenders build, validate, and deploy application, behavioural, and collection scorecards in hours instead of months. Backed by Accel. No public self-service developer API is published; API integration is enterprise/sales-gated.

## Company
- [Website](https://www.finbots.ai/): Trusted AI credit risk platform (CreditX, Decision Engine, propensity modelling, managed services).
- [CreditX Products](https://www.finbots.ai/creditx/): Application, behavioural, and collection scorecards built with proprietary explainable AI.
- [Decision Engine](https://www.finbots.ai/business-rules-engine/): Business rules engine for automated lending decisions.
- [Case Studies](https://www.finbots.ai/case-studies/): Deployments across APAC and MEA banks and lenders.
- [Press](https://www.finbots.ai/press/): Announcements and press releases.
- [Our Team](https://www.finbots.ai/company/team/): Leadership and team.
- [Contact](https://www.finbots.ai/contact/): Demo requests and support.
